How the brain learns

The longer you use it, the more it's yours.

Four things happen quietly in the background. A blank prompt does none of them.

01

It hears you

Two minutes of you talking, and it writes in your rhythm, not a description of it. Your sentence length, the way you join a thought, what you call things. Every voice note you leave afterwards teaches it more.

2 min · that's the voice
02

It reads your brand

Paste your website and it reads the whole thing, then your competitors, your reviews, your social posts. You approve what it keeps. Nothing lands in the brain unseen.

03

It learns from every choice

Approve, dismiss, change a line. It keeps the pattern behind the choice, not just the post, and turns it into a rule every tool obeys. You're training it just by using it.

"Our AMAZING formula!!!" → "1000mg sodium. Zero sugar."
✓ Rule: state it flat, cut the hype
04

It compounds

Week ten sounds more like you than week one. It reads what you kept and what you rejected, writes the pattern into your Voice Memory, and applies it from that moment on. A blank prompt starts from zero every time.

Questions

The honest answers

What's a "brand brain," really? +
Everything it knows about you, in one place: what you sell, who it's for, how you talk (it heard you), and everything it learns from how you work — the lines you keep, the ones you rewrite, the ones you pass on. Every post is written through it. It's memory, not just generation.
How fast until I have posts? +
Paste your website — it fills most of your profile in seconds. Answer a couple of quick questions to sharpen it, then hit generate. About two minutes to your first batch, ready to film.
Do I have to fill out a huge form? +
No. Paste your website and it fills most of it. Then talk for two minutes about what you do, the way you'd explain it to a friend. That's the part that makes it sound like you. Add anything else by hand whenever you like; from there it learns from the posts you approve, edit and pass.
What kinds of posts does it make? +
Six: talking-head scripts, statements, carousels, micro-lectures, Q&As and statics. You set the mix once (say, three videos, two carousels, two statements a week) and it plans the week around it. Change the mix any time.
What if I have nothing to say? +
That's the point of it. The ideas come from your brand, from what people search, from what's rising in your niche, from posts by creators you follow, and from the half-thoughts you say into the notebook. You're one source of five, so it never runs dry.
Do I need a computer, or does it work on my phone? +
Your phone is all you need. It's built mobile-first — write, review and post right from your thumb, nothing to install. Works just as well on a desktop if you prefer, but you're never tied to one.
Will it actually sound like me? +
Yes, because it heard you. During setup you talk for two minutes, and that recording outranks every description and every rule when it writes. Every voice note you leave afterwards teaches it more, and every post you approve or change sharpens it further.
