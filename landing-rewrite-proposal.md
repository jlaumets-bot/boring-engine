## 2. NEW — What comes out

**H2:** Six kinds of post. One brain.

Six small cards in a row (mono icon + title + one line):

- **Talking-head script** — written to be said, not read. Film it with the teleprompter.
- **Statement** — one bold claim, made for a text graphic.
- **Carousel** — slide by slide, ready for the slide maker.
- **Micro-lecture** — one surprising fact, under a minute.
- **Q&A** — a real question people search, answered your way.
- **Static** — caption plus image brief.

Line under the row:
> Set your mix once. Three videos, two carousels, two statements a week, whatever fits you. It plans around it, and it never repeats itself.

*(Bonus format left out on purpose; it's a day, not a kind of post.)*

## 3. NEW — Where the ideas come from

**H2:** It never runs dry, because you're not the only source.

- **Your brand.** What you sell, who it's for, what annoys them, what you'd never say.
- **What people search.** Real questions from Google, one tap to answer.
- **What's rising in your niche.** Pulled daily, with the posts worth making yours.
- **Creators you follow.** Paste a post that worked. Get it rebuilt in your voice, never copied.
- **Your own half-thoughts.** Say it into the notebook at a red light. It becomes a post later.

## 4. Learn section — 3 cards → 4

**H2 — keep:** The longer you use it, the more it's yours.

1. **It hears you.** *(new, first)* Two minutes of you talking and it writes in your rhythm, not a description of it. Every voice note you leave teaches it more.
2. **It reads your brand.** Your site, your socials, your reviews, your competitors. You approve what it keeps.
3. **It learns from every choice.** Approve, dismiss, change a line. Each one is a lesson it doesn't forget.
4. **It compounds.** Week ten sounds more like you than week one. A blank prompt starts from zero every time.

## 5. Feature grid — 22 → 8

**H2 — keep:** Everything between "I should post" and "it's live."

1. **Quick Post** — one tap. Today's post, in your voice, in about thirty seconds.
2. **Week planner** — seven days across your formats and themes. Approve, film, done.
3. **Film it** — a teleprompter that follows your voice, then a split-screen video with on-brand graphics and photos, built on your phone. No editor.
4. **Remix** — a link or a transcript in, your version out.
5. **Trends** — what's rising in your niche, and what your competitors just did.
6. **What people search** — real questions, answered as posts.
7. **Assistant** — talk it out. It knows your brand and what you've approved.
8. **Multi-brand + team** — separate brains, separate voices, one login.

One line under the grid replaces four cards:
> Works on your phone. Frontier models underneath. Useful the first day.
