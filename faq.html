Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask before they start.

Why does Content Shrimp exist? Because getting on-brand content out of ChatGPT means prompting, re-explaining your brand in every new chat, arguing with it, reminding it, and keeping a separate conversation for every tool — hours of babysitting for hit-and-miss results. Here, your brand lives in one permanent brand brain and every tool writes through it. No prompts. No re-explaining. Open, tap, post.
The basics
How is Content Shrimp different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is a blank general-purpose chat. To get on-brand content out of it you end up prompt-engineering, re-explaining your brand in every new conversation, correcting it, reminding it, and keeping a separate chat for every type of content — and after hours of babysitting, the results are still hit-and-miss. Content Shrimp is the opposite: your brand lives in a permanent brand brain — voice, audience, product facts, competitors, what you approve and how you edit — and every tool (posts, scripts, remixes, memes) writes through it automatically. No prompts, no re-explaining, no chat-per-tool. Open, tap, post.
Do I need to be a good writer to use this?
Not at all. You fill in fields about your brand — or let it scan your website and fill them for you — and Content Shrimp generates complete scripts, captions, hooks, and shot lists. If you can describe your business, you can use it.
What kind of content does it generate?
Full content briefs mapped to a 7-day calendar — short-form video scripts, carousels, statement graphics, micro-content, Q&A reels and memes. Each brief includes hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags, and shot lists so you can film the same day.
Which AI model writes my content?
Grok, xAI's newest model — but the model is just the writer. Everything it writes goes through your brand brain: the same stored voice, product facts, and learned rules every single time. That's why the output stays consistent — the memory is yours and permanent; the engine simply reads it.
The brand brain
What is the brand brain?
It is the memory that makes every output sound like you instead of generic AI. It stores your full brand profile — voice and tone, product facts, audience, pain points, the exact phrases you use, words you never use, your CTA style, origin story, and visual style — and feeds all of it into every single thing it writes. Nothing is generated from a blank slate.
Does the brand brain actually learn, or is it static?
It learns. Every time you approve an idea, pass on one and say why, or edit a line, that signal is distilled into durable voice-memory rules and saved. The more you use it, the sharper it gets at sounding like you — and that memory is applied across every tool, not just where you taught it.
Won't it just produce generic AI content like other tools?
No. Generic tools start cold every time. Content Shrimp starts with your complete brand profile plus everything it has learned from your feedback, baked into every generation. It also follows explicit rules against the tells that make AI writing obvious — no filler, no hype, no rule-of-three padding. Specific and on-brand, not beige.
What are trends and how long do they last?
When you find a video that is blowing up, you can teach your brand the trend in Trends. It saves the mechanic, not the topic, and shapes your next ideas for about two months, then expires on its own so stale trends never linger. You decide what is worth riding.

Yes. Your brand voice data, content ideas, and team information are stored securely and only accessible to you and your invited team members. We don't share your data with other users or use it to train AI models.
Is my brand brain shared with other users or used to train AI?
Never. Your brand profile, learned voice memory, and content are yours alone — visible only to you and the team you invite. We do not pool it with other brands and we do not use it to train AI models.
